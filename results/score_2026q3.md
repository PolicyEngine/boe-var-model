## Scope

- Only the UK legs are scored. World GDP, world CPI and oil are built from FRED and OECD series (`data/README.md`), outside the publishers approved for this download (ONS, BoE, ECB/Eurostat, Destatis).
- One quarter is one draw from each predictive distribution. It says where the outturn fell, nothing about calibration or skill; the backtest in `results/coverage_evaluation.json` is the evidence for that.
- First estimates revise. Re-score when ONS revises 2026Q3.
