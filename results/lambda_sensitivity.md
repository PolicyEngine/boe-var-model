Generated by `scripts/run_lambda_sensitivity.py`; do not edit by hand. Issues #11 and #12.

- Published specification (six Covid dummies), 50 expanding-window origins, h1–h8, 1,000 posterior draws × 5 paths per origin, seed 20260729, data 1992Q1–2026Q2.
- The Minnesota tightness λ is the only thing that changes. The published model uses λ = 0.2; 1.04 is the marginal-likelihood optimum.
- Coverage is of the raw model bands, before any calibration factor. With 50 overlapping origins the standard error of a coverage figure is about 0.04 at 90% and 0.07 at 68% for a single cell.
