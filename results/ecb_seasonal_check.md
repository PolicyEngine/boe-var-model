# World CPI seasonal adjustment: #34's filter against the ECB's

Generated by `scripts/compare_ecb_seasonal.py` from committed files: the unadjusted proxy (`data/boe_var_data_world_cpi_nsa.csv`), the adjusted proxy (`data/boe_var_data.csv`), and the ECB's working-day and seasonally adjusted euro-area HICP with Eurostat's unadjusted index (`data/external/ecb_hicp_euro_area_monthly.csv`; provenance in `data/external/README.md`). Window 1997Q2-2025Q2, 113 quarters: from the ECB series' start to the last quarter in which all four legs of the proxy have data. Quarterly log growth, 100*log points.

The ECB variant replaces only the euro-area leg's seasonal component (weight 0.643-0.694, renormalised over the four legs as in `TRADE_WEIGHT_ERAS`).

## Reading

- Both adjustments pass the seasonal gate in every era, and they remove nearly the same pattern, including its drift (a larger Q2 effect since 2015). The source adjustment independently supports #34's finding that the unadjusted proxy carries a seasonal the VAR does not absorb.
- The ECB's component is somewhat larger in amplitude (it also removes calendar effects, and the proxy's euro-area leg is the EA19 index, not the changing-composition euro area).
- The production column is not switched: the gap in year-on-year inflation is small against the model's bands, a switch would need the German backcast and the China leg adjusted at source too, and it would regenerate every artefact. Revisit at the next data refresh.
